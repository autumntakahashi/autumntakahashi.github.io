A post to show formatted math to see my setup works.

Inline math goes between double dollar signs, like $$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$ in the middle of a sentence, or a variable with a subscript such as $$x_i$$.

Display math goes on its own lines:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

Multi-line equations work with `aligned`:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} &= \frac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0} \\
\nabla \times \mathbf{B} &= \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \varepsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}
\end{aligned}
$$

And matrices:

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} \\ a_{21} & a_{22} \end{pmatrix}
$$
